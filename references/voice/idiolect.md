# Idiolect

An idiolect is the recurring way a particular writer uses language.

VozNativa may observe from user-provided samples:
- sentence-length distribution;
- paragraph shape;
- punctuation habits;
- recurring and avoided lexicon;
- discourse openers/connectors;
- contractions;
- abbreviations;
- foreign terms;
- emoji frequency;
- capitalization;
- self-correction;
- channel-specific variation.

Precedence: author sample > channel/context > community > region > generic pt-BR.

Do not infer age, gender, ethnicity, class, diagnosis, location or personality from these observations.
