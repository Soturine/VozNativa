# False-positive guard

Do not treat these as AI evidence by themselves:
- correct grammar;
- formal vocabulary;
- a single connector;
- a single em dash;
- repeated terminology required by the domain;
- passive voice in technical/legal genres;
- consistent formatting from a template;
- regional marker present in the author's sample;
- terse prose;
- explicit lists when the content is genuinely enumerative.

A candidate edit needs contextual evidence, not keyword presence.
